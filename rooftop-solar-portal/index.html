Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for the rooftop solar subsidy?

Any residential consumer with a valid electricity connection and suitable roof, subject to DISCOM feasibility approval.

How much subsidy will I get?

₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW — maximum ₹78,000 for a 3 kW or larger residential system.

How long does the process take?

Typically 60–90 days end to end, including approval, installation, net metering and subsidy disbursal via DBT.
